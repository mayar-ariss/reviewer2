Here's the abstract of my clinical trial paper. Can you review it?

"Background: Knee osteoarthritis is a leading cause of disability. Methods: In a randomized trial, 212 adults with knee osteoarthritis received either a 12-week supervised exercise programme or usual care. The primary outcome was pain at 12 weeks (WOMAC pain subscale). Results: Pain at 12 weeks did not differ between groups (mean difference −0.8 points, 95% CI −2.1 to 0.5; p = 0.21). Physical function improved more in the exercise group (p = 0.03). Conclusion: The supervised exercise programme is an effective treatment for knee osteoarthritis and should be offered routinely."
