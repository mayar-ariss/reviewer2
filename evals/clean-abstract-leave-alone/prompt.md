"Urban ponds are increasingly used as stormwater basins, but their value as amphibian breeding habitat is poorly known. We surveyed 48 ponds in Leeds, UK, in spring 2023 and 2024, recording breeding by four amphibian species and measuring water quality and surrounding land cover. Breeding occurred in 31 ponds (65%). Ponds with less than 25% impervious cover within 100 m were 3.2 times more likely to host breeding (95% CI 1.6–6.4), while conductivity had no detectable effect. These results suggest that the land around stormwater ponds, rather than their water chemistry, limits their use by amphibians, and that planning rules for new basins could include a vegetated buffer."
