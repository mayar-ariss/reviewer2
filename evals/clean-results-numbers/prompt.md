Please check the numbers in this paragraph from the Results of my transport paper:

"Across 1,240 matched weekday trips, median door-to-door commute time fell from 34 min (interquartile range 27–41) before the bus lanes opened to 29 min (23–36) after, a reduction of 5 min (95% CI 3–7). The reduction was larger on routes that used the lanes for more than half their length (7 min, 95% CI 4–10) than on the others (2 min, 95% CI 0–4). A 5-minute saving is about one sixth of the 30-minute threshold the city uses to define a long commute."
